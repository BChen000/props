Meet the Griffins: A Baby Griffin's First Flight

High above the clouds, a griffin chick hatches on a narrow cliff ledge. It's all fluff, oversized paws and wings far too small to fly with, yet within a year it will take its first leap from the cliff and start becoming one of the most feared hunters in the sky.

Species: Griffin (Gryps montanus)
